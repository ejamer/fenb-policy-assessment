User is building an Obsidian governance analysis vault for Fencing-Escrime NB (FENB), a provincial fencing association incorporated in NB (2006). Source documents are in `/home/edjamer/Documents/GitHub/hugo-testing/fenb-1/` — DO NOT EDIT those files.

Vault is at `/home/edjamer/Documents/Obsidian-Vault/FENB Governance/`.

**Why:** To assess existing policies and procedures; identify gaps; understand governance architecture.

**Structure created (June 2026):**
- 00 - Home: Hub/MOC note
- 01 - Foundation: Bylaws, Board Policy Manual (Carver), Strategic Plan 2024-2028
- 02 - Policies: 12 active policies + Policy Index + Concussion Protocol
- 03 - Analysis: 6 analysis notes covering architecture, gaps, cross-references, strategic alignment, ED role
- 04 - Forms: Complaint Intake Form (from Policy 2.18 annex)

**Key findings documented in vault:**
1. Stale cross-references: Policies 2.11 and 2.17 reference "Communications and Social Media Policy (Section 2.1)" — that standalone no longer exists; it was consolidated into the new 2.1 Safe Sport policy in April 2026
2. Policy 2.13 ("Safe Sport and Athlete Welfare") was merged into 2.1 but 2.17 still references 2.13
3. Numbering gaps: 2.4 (Equipment/School Programs — absent but referenced), 2.8, 2.9, 2.15, 2.16
4. Carver monitoring schedule (Board Policy Manual p.12) is disconnected from operational policies
5. ED defined three different ways: Bylaws (narrow admin role), Carver Board Policy Manual (full operational authority), 12 operational policies (20+ specific accountabilities)
6. Strategic Plan Strategies 4 and 5 (Leadership/Infrastructure, Athlete Development) have almost no policy support
7. The archived Operations Manual in the source repo has not been read — may contain Crisis Management Protocol predecessor
8. FENB Pay Scale is in Google Docs (external, unversioned)

**How to apply:** When working in this vault, follow the CLAUDE.md conventions for frontmatter, tagging, and the distinction between policy notes (include full text + Observations section) and analysis notes (interpretive).
