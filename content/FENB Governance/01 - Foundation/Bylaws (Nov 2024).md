# Bylaws — Fencing-Escrime NB Inc.

*Amended November 2024. Incorporated by letters patent, February 2006.*

---

## Membership Structure

### Individual Members (§5)

| Type | Competition Eligibility | Voting Rights |
|------|------------------------|---------------|
| Fencing Member | Full — provincial, national, international | Yes |
| Recreational Fencing Member | Practice only; can upgrade during season | Yes |
| Special Recreational Fencing Member | Designated program only; can upgrade | **No** |
| Associate Member (non-fencer: coach, referee, volunteer, etc.) | Provincial clinics only | Yes |
| Life Member | Honorary, Board-bestowed | Yes |

> Note: Fencing Members cannot downgrade to Recreational during the season.

### Group Members (§6)

- **Club** — 6+ members; instructional programs require a Head Coach certified at Community Initiation or Instructor-Beginner level
- **Affiliate** — Non-profit associations promoting fencing in NB
- **Commercial** — Companies contributing annually to FENB objectives

---

## Meetings of Members

| Meeting Type | Trigger | Notice Required | Quorum |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|--------|
| AGM | Within 180 days of June 30 FYE | 21 days | 10 voting members |
| Special GM | 3 directors OR 40% of voting members | 10 days | 10 voting members |

- **Voting**: One vote per eligible member; members under 13 vote through parent/guardian; 13–15 may delegate
- **Proxy**: Up to 3 proxies per member; requires signed letter
- **Decisions**: Simple majority; show of hands unless ballot motion passes
- **Electronic voting**: President may submit questions electronically; decision by majority within 14 days (§26)
- **Board overrule**: Business implemented by Board may be overruled by a general meeting (§27)

## Board of Directors

### Composition (§16)
Seven directors: President + Athlete Development Representative + 5 Directors at Large

### Election (§21)
- 2-year staggered terms
- Year A: President + 3 directors at large
- Year B: 3 directors at large
- Must be registered members in good standing

### Specific Roles

**President (§17)**
- Chairs Board and AGM
- Co-signs all official documents with any other Director
- Appoints or serves as CFF branch representative and Sport NB representative
- Files year-end report to membership at AGM

**Athlete Development Representative (§18)**
- Recommends policies for athlete development
- Sits on Athlete Development Committee (if one exists)
- CFF liaison on athlete development

**Executive Director (§19)**
- Keeps records of all meetings
- Has charge of books, records, and correspondence
- Has custody of the Association seal
- Co-signs with the President all official documents

### Board Operations
- Minimum 3 Board meetings per year; may be in-person, teleconference, or electronic (§22)
- Quorum: majority of directors (4 of 7) (§23)
- One vote each; tie broken by chairperson (§24)
- Vacancy filled by Board majority vote appointment (§25)

### Termination of Tenure (§30–32)
- Automatic vacation: written resignation OR absence from **2 consecutive Board meetings**
- Removal: 2/3 vote at special meeting; both parties may present case
- President may suspend any officer or member; suspended member may appeal

---

## Financial Provisions

- **Fiscal year**: July 1 – June 30 (§33)
- **Membership fees**: Set by Board; fee changes require majority vote at GM (§7)
- **Signing authority**: 2 of 3 designated signatories; one must be ED or President; third is a Board-designated director (§34)

---

## Structural Rules

- **Bylaws amendment**: 21-day notice to members; 2/3 of votes cast at GM (§35)
- **Dissolution**: 30-day notice; 90% of votes present; assets to registered charity with similar objects (§37)
- **Parliamentary procedure**: Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised, latest edition (§36)
- **Irregularities**: Acts of Board valid even if a director's appointment was defective (§28)
- **Remuneration**: No director receives remuneration for Board duties (§29)

## Observations

### Bylaw–Carver mismatch #gap
The ED's role in §19 is described narrowly: records, books, seal, co-signature. The [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]] (Carver model) grants the ED full operational authority over everything not explicitly prohibited. This mismatch is legally benign under the Carver framework but creates ambiguity — a strict reading of the Bylaws would not grant the ED the decision-making power the Board Policy Manual intends. The Bylaws may need a broader ED clause to reflect actual governance practice.

### Special Recreational membership has no voting rights #gap
The Special Recreational category (§5c) — likely the school program — now represents 68 of 289 members (23%) as of 2023-24, the fastest-growing segment. This category has no voting rights. This may create a democratic legitimacy concern as school programs grow, and potentially conflicts with the [[2.3 Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion]] policy's commitment to inclusive participation.

### Athlete Development Committee not named in Bylaws #gap
§18 says the Athlete Development Representative "sits as a member of an athlete development committee, if one exists" — leaving committee formation entirely discretionary. The [[Strategic Plan 2024-2028]] treats the ADC as a significant vehicle for Strategy 5, but there is no policy framework governing its mandate, composition, or authority.

### 40% special meeting threshold is accessible
At 40% of voting members needed to call a Special GM (§10), a significant minority can force a meeting. With ~289 members (many of whom are voting-eligible), the practical number is approximately 116 members. This is worth noting for governance resilience.

### Signing authority safeguard is solid
§34 requires 2 of 3 signatories, with one being ED or President. This dual-signatory control is reinforced in [[2.6 Financial Management]] and effectively prevents any single actor from authorizing financial transactions.
