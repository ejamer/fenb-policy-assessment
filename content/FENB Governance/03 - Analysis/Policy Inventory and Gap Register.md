## Gap Analysis

### Section 2.4 — Equipment and School Program Policy
**Evidence of existence:** Explicitly referenced in [[2.5 Official Language]] ("Equipment and School Program Policy (Section 2.4)").  
**Why it matters:** The school program is FENB's fastest-growing membership category (0 → 68 members in one year). These members have Special Recreational status with no voting rights and participate in what appears to be a FENB-sponsored program distinct from club membership. The policy governing equipment use, program standards, and liability for school programs is a meaningful gap.  
**Likely content:** Equipment lending/use standards, school program delivery requirements, insurance coverage for school participants, supervision requirements, program design standards.

### Sections 2.8 and 2.9 — Unknown
**Evidence of existence:** None found in any cross-reference. The numbering gap between 2.7 (HR) and 2.10 (Volunteer Management) suggests two policies that either:
- Existed previously and were removed or merged (like 2.13)
- Were planned but not yet drafted
- Were never numbered and the gap is an artifact of the numbering scheme

**Candidate subjects for 2.8/2.9:**
- **Communications Policy** — The former standalone Communications and Social Media Policy that was merged into 2.1. It may have originally held a 2.8 or 2.9 number before being renumbered as 2.1.
- **Membership or Registration Policy** — No policy currently governs how membership is administered, fees are collected, or what happens if someone's membership lapses. The Bylaws address membership categories; the operational policies don't.
- **Club Governance Standards** — No policy governs what affiliated clubs must do, how they operate, or what standards they must meet. The Board Policy Manual EDB-A provisions imply minimum standards, but there's no operational policy.

### Sections 2.15 and 2.16 — Unknown
**Evidence of existence:** None found. The gap between 2.14 (Conflict of Interest) and 2.17 (Crisis and Emergency Management) suggests two further policies.

**Candidate subjects for 2.15/2.16:**
- **Athlete Development Policy** — The [[Strategic Plan 2024-2028]] (Strategy 5) is heavily focused on athlete development, HP programs, and provincial team support. The Athlete Development Representative is a designated Board position (Bylaws §18). No policy framework governs this domain.
- **Selection and Eligibility Policy** — The Board Policy Manual (EDB-A4-C and EDB-A4-D) implies that selection criteria must be clear and fair. No standalone policy governs selection for teams, programs, or competitions.
- **Communications and Media Policy** — As a standalone (formerly 2.1 before consolidation into the new 2.1), it might have been renumbered to a higher section.
- **Event and Competition Policy** — How provincial events are sanctioned, what standards they must meet, and what the technical committee does is not covered by any current policy.
