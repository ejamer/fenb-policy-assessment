# 2.14 Conflict of Interest and Ethics Policy

**Approved April 2026**

## Purpose
Ensure decisions and actions by FENB representatives are made with integrity, objectivity, and transparency, free from conflicts between personal interests and organizational responsibilities. Promotes ethical behaviour, accountability, and public trust in FENB's governance and operations.

## Scope
All FENB Board members, staff, contractors, volunteers, coaches, and officials who participate in decision-making or represent the organization in any capacity.

## Key Provisions

**Definition of Conflict of Interest**
A conflict of interest arises when an individual's personal, professional, or financial interests may compromise (or appear to compromise) their impartiality, judgment, or ability to act in FENB's best interest. Conflicts may be real, potential, or perceived. Includes situations where the individual or their immediate family members receive or stand to receive benefit from an FENB decision, contract, or opportunity.

**Disclosure Requirements**
- All covered individuals must disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts as soon as they arise
- **Annual conflict-of-interest declarations** required of all Board members and senior staff; retained by ED
- When a conflict arises during a meeting: declare immediately; withdraw from discussion and decision-making on that matter

**Gifts, Benefits, and Favours**
- FENB members shall not solicit or accept gifts, benefits, or hospitality that could reasonably be perceived to influence professional judgment or create an obligation
- Modest hospitality and tokens of appreciation (plaques, sport memorabilia) may be accepted when customary and not linked to any advantage or decision
- Any gift exceeding nominal value must be disclosed to ED for review and entry into a **gift log**

**Outside Activities and Employment**
- No external positions or business interests conflicting with FENB's operations, commitments, or reputation
- Outside employment/consulting must not interfere with organizational duties or create divided loyalties

**Decision-Making Integrity**
- Procurement, hiring, and funding decisions must be made impartially
- Board ensures all major financial and contractual decisions adhere to this policy and [[2.6 Financial Management]]

**Reporting and Compliance**
- Alleged breaches reported to ED or, if ED is implicated, to the President of the Board
- Reported conflicts reviewed confidentially; resolution may include recusal, suspension, or removal from position
- FENB may refer serious ethical breaches to external authorities

**Recordkeeping and Transparency**
- FENB maintains a **register of disclosed conflicts and gifts**, accessible to the Board
- Meeting minutes document declarations of conflict and subsequent recusals
- ED reviews all declarations annually and provides summary report to the Board

**Ethical Conduct**
- All FENB representatives expected to uphold highest standards of honesty, respect, and accountability per [[2.1 Safe Sport, Conduct, and Athlete Protection]]
- Decisions must always prioritize the best interests of the organization and the integrity of the sport

## Observations

### Aligns with and reinforces Carver GP-D1
This policy operationalizes the Board Code of Conduct requirement (GP-D1) in [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]] that directors complete annual Conflict of Interest Declarations. The Board Policy Manual requires this; this policy defines what it means and how it's managed.

### "Gift exceeding nominal value" is undefined
The policy requires disclosure to the ED of any gift "exceeding a nominal value" but does not define that threshold. Many similar policies specify a dollar amount (e.g., $25 or $50). Without a defined threshold, application will be inconsistent.

### The conflict-of-interest register is an important oversight tool
The requirement to maintain a register of disclosed conflicts and gifts, accessible to the Board, is a strong governance provision. It creates a documented accountability trail. However, its practical implementation depends on the ED maintaining it consistently — and there is no stated consequence for failure to disclose other than the general disciplinary framework.

### "Board Governance and Accountability Guidelines" may not exist
The Related Policies section references "Board Governance and Accountability Guidelines (if applicable)." The "(if applicable)" qualifier suggests this document may not exist. If it does, it should be identified and added to this vault. If it doesn't, the reference should be removed at next review.
