## Observations

### Insurance review is annual but no minimum coverage threshold is specified
The policy requires annual insurance review but does not specify minimum coverage amounts for general liability, D&O, or accident coverage. Clubs and event organizers submitting "documentation confirming coverage" have no baseline to meet. Setting minimum coverage floors (likely aligned with CFF or Sport NB requirements) would strengthen this.

### Directors' and officers' insurance is explicitly required
The explicit mention of D&O coverage is important and appropriate for a Carver-governed Board where directors have potentially personal liability exposure. This coverage protects the Board members who serve without remuneration.

### "Registered members in good standing" requirement for club events is a compliance mechanism
Requiring that all participants in club events be registered FENB members in good standing is how the insurance coverage flows from the provincial association to the club level. The practical enforcement of this requirement (i.e., how clubs verify registration) is not addressed in this or any other policy.

## Summary

### Purpose
Ensure FENB maintains adequate insurance coverage and complies with all applicable legal and regulatory requirements. Protects the organization, members, and stakeholders from financial and legal risks. Supports responsible governance aligned with risk management, accountability, and safe participation.

### Scope
All FENB programs, events, and operations including those delivered through affiliated clubs or partner organizations. Covers insurance, contracts, compliance with laws and regulations, and related governance obligations.

### Key Provisions

**Insurance Coverage**
- FENB will maintain comprehensive coverage including:
  - General liability
  - Directors' and officers' liability
  - Property
  - Accident
  - Event insurance
- ED, in consultation with Board, reviews coverage **annually**
- Proof of insurance provided to affiliated clubs and venues upon request; renewed certificates kept on file

**Club and Event Insurance**
- All sanctioned tournaments, clinics, and programs must have current insurance through FENB or an approved provider
- Affiliated clubs required to maintain their own insurance and ensure all participants are registered members in good standing
- Event organizers must submit documentation confirming venue insurance, safety compliance, and adherence to [[2.2 Risk Management]]

**Legal and Regulatory Compliance**
- FENB will comply with all relevant federal, provincial, and municipal laws: occupational health and safety, privacy, employment, and not-for-profit legislation
- All contracts, waivers, and partnership agreements must be reviewed and approved by ED or Board before execution
- Documentation and records retained per [[2.11 Privacy and Data Protection]] and [[2.6 Financial Management]]

**Incident Reporting and Claims**
- Any incident resulting in injury, property damage, or potential liability must be reported **immediately** to the ED
- ED completes required insurance forms and notifies insurer within specified timeframe
- All incident reports documented and reviewed per [[2.1 Safe Sport, Conduct, and Athlete Protection]] and [[2.17 Crisis and Emergency Management]]

**Training and Awareness**
- Information sessions for Board members, staff, and club leaders on insurance and legal responsibilities
- Annual orientation includes review of insurance requirements and risk management procedures
