## Observations

### The risk register is an ED deliverable with no defined format #gap
The policy requires the ED to maintain an annual risk register but does not specify its format, contents, or what constitutes "significant" risk. For a small organization without a dedicated risk function, an explicit risk register template would strengthen this policy's practical application. This vault could host a working version.

### Risk appetite statement is qualitative
The statement that FENB has "low tolerance for risks that may compromise participant safety, safeguarding, legal compliance, or ethical standards" is important but doesn't address financial or reputational risk tolerance. The [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]] (EDB-B) implies low tolerance for financial jeopardy, but neither document establishes a full risk appetite matrix.

## Summary

### Purpose
Ensure FENB identifies, assesses, and manages potential risks affecting operations, reputation, financial integrity, and participant safety. Supports sound decision-making and safeguards FENB's people, assets, and programs.

### Scope
All FENB activities: tournaments, programs, training, governance, partnerships. Covers physical safety, financial controls, data protection, and reputational risk. Applies to Board, ED, staff, volunteers, officials, coaches, athletes, affiliated clubs.

### Key Provisions

**Risk Management Framework**
- FENB maintains a proactive and systematic approach to identifying, analyzing, and responding to risks
- Risk management is integral to organizational decision-making, planning, and program delivery
- **ED is designated Risk Manager** — logs, monitors, and reports all significant risks to the Board
- Board retains overall responsibility for risk oversight; approves annual risk register and mitigation strategies

**Risk Categories**
- Strategic — governance, planning, or compliance failures
- Operational — health, safety, or logistical issues at events
- Financial — fraud, budget variance, or funding loss
- Reputational — misconduct, social-media incidents, or data breaches
- Compliance — failure to meet laws, insurance, or policy obligations

**Risk Assessment**
- Risks assessed based on likelihood and potential impact; prioritized in the risk register
- Input gathered from staff, coaches, clubs, and external partners

**Risk Mitigation**
- Each identified risk has a mitigation plan: owner, response strategy, timeline
- Strategies: avoidance, reduction, transfer (insurance), or acceptance
- **FENB has a low tolerance for risks that may compromise participant safety, safeguarding, legal compliance, or ethical standards**
- Insurance coverage per [[2.12 Insurance and Legal Compliance]]
- Safety risks at events addressed in coordination with [[2.1 Safe Sport, Conduct, and Athlete Protection]] and [[2.17 Crisis and Emergency Management]]

**Monitoring and Reporting**
- ED reviews and updates risk register at least annually and after major changes
- Material risks or incidents reported to Board immediately; follow-up actions documented
- Lessons learned integrated into updated mitigation strategies

**Shared Responsibility**
- While Board and ED hold oversight, risk management is a shared responsibility
- All FENB representatives expected to identify, mitigate, and report risks within their scope

**Training**
- Training for staff, volunteers, clubs on identifying and reporting risks
- New Board/committee member orientation includes risk management framework overview
