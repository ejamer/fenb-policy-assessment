## Observations

### Pay scale is an external annex — not a controlled document in the vault #gap
The FENB Pay Scale is held in a Google Docs document. This means it can be changed without the formal policy review process, and it may not be accessible to all members. For a policy committed to transparency in compensation (section 8), housing the actual scales outside the governance document set is a governance gap. The pay scale should at minimum be versioned and dated, and changes should require the same authorization level as policy changes.

### Distinction between 2.7 and 2.10 is blurry for coaches
The HR policy (2.7) covers "staff, contractors, coaches, officials, and volunteers." The Volunteer Management policy (2.10) also covers coaches and officials. Coaches at clubs occupy an ambiguous category — they may be volunteers or paid contractors. The two policies should more clearly delineate which applies to which category.

### No succession planning framework
Strategy 4 of the [[Strategic Plan 2024-2028]] explicitly names succession planning as a priority. The HR policy does not address succession planning for the ED or Board roles. This is a gap given that the Strategic Plan identified coach transitions at Damocles as a specific organizational vulnerability.

## Summary

### Purpose
Ensure FENB recruits, develops, supports, and manages staff, contractors, coaches, officials, and volunteers fairly, consistently, and professionally. Establishes expectations for hiring, conduct, supervision, workplace practices, performance management, compensation, and conflict resolution per Sport NB standards and NB employment requirements.

### Scope
All FENB staff (full-time, part-time, contract), Board members, volunteers, and affiliated personnel delivering programs, services, or events.

### Key Provisions

**1. Recruitment and Selection**
- Open, fair, transparent hiring and engagement processes
- Public job postings wherever possible
- Selection based on job-related criteria, experience, and FENB values alignment
- Job descriptions approved by ED; reviewed regularly
- Equitable hiring practices per DEI and Gender Equity policies

**2. Screening and Background Checks**
- Positions of trust require: references, criminal record checks, vulnerable sector checks
- Vulnerable sector checks renewed **every 3 years** or more frequently if required by governing bodies
- Administered confidentially by ED
- All personnel must complete **Safe Sport training prior to assuming duties**

**3. Orientation and Supervision**
- Orientation covers FENB policies, Safe Sport standards, reporting mechanisms, workplace expectations
- Supervisors ensure compliance with **Rule of Two principles**
- Workers follow workplace safety practices and reporting requirements

**4. Hours of Work**
- Standard weekly hours per NB Employment Standards; defined in employment agreements
- Flexible or remote work arrangements may be approved by ED
- Break periods per NB Employment Standards Act

**5. Overtime**
- Must be pre-approved by ED
- Compensated through payment or time-in-lieu per NB Employment Standards Act minimums

**6. Leaves of Absence**
FENB recognizes all statutory NB leaves:
- sick leave, bereavement leave, parental/maternity leave, family responsibility leave
- domestic violence leave, jury duty, compassionate care leave
- Additional unpaid or short-term leaves at FENB's discretion

**7. Expenses**
- Pre-approved expenses reimbursed with receipts
- Claims submitted **within 30 days**
- ED or Treasurer must approve all reimbursements

**8. Compensation Scale**
- Transparent compensation framework with salary ranges or standardized contract/honoraria rates
- Pay periods, vacation pay, holidays, and benefits per NB Employment Standards
- Adjustments based on performance, funding levels, or market comparisons
- Full scale in external annex: [FENB Pay Scale — Google Docs]

**9. Performance Management and Evaluation**
- Annual performance evaluations: job performance, Safe Sport compliance, contributions to organizational goals
- Contractors and key volunteers receive periodic reviews
- Improvement plans implemented where necessary
- Professional development opportunities supported

**10. Conduct and Discipline**
- Must comply with Code of Conduct, Anti-Harassment Policy, Safe Sport Policy, and DEI commitments
- Progressive discipline: verbal warning → written warning → suspension → termination
- **UCCMS-related maltreatment allegations directed to NBSSCM** — not handled internally
- FENB may take immediate protective/administrative measures separate from external investigations

**11. Privacy and Confidentiality**
- HR records stored securely; restricted to authorized personnel
- Personal information collected, used, retained per NB privacy law
- Screening and disciplinary information confidential unless legally required to disclose

**12. Termination**
- May occur with or without cause per NB Employment Standards Act
- Required notice or pay in lieu
- All FENB property returned upon termination
- Exit interview may be offered

**13. Grievance and Conflict Resolution**
- Formal grievances submitted in writing; reviewed by ED or designated Board member
- Appeals may be made to Board of Directors
- Retaliation for raising a grievance is prohibited
- **UCCMS-related complaints directed to NBSSCM, not handled internally**

**14. Official Languages**
FENB will respect principles of the NB Official Languages Act in communications and service delivery wherever feasible.

**15. Review**
Every 2 years or sooner for compliance with NB employment law, Sport NB requirements, or Safe Sport standards.
