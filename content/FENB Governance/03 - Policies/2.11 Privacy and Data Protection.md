## Observations

### Stale cross-reference to "Communications and Social Media Policy (Section 2.1)" #inconsistency
In the "Related Policies" section, this policy references "Communications and Social Media Policy (Section 2.1)." Section 2.1 is the Safe Sport, Conduct, and Athlete Protection Policy, which now *includes* the communications and social media provisions. The label implies a separate, standalone policy that no longer exists. This is one of two instances of this inconsistency in the policy manual (the other is in [[2.17 Crisis and Emergency Management]]). See [[Policy Cross-Reference Map]].

### NB-specific privacy legislation should be named
The policy references "NB privacy law" and "applicable privacy legislation" without specifying which Act applies. New Brunswick's Right to Information and Protection of Privacy Act (RTIPPA) applies to public bodies; FENB as a private NFP likely falls under PIPEDA (federal) or sector-specific rules. Being explicit about which legislation governs would strengthen the policy.

### "Reasonable timeframe" for access requests is vague
The right to access personal information response within "a reasonable timeframe" is undefined. PIPEDA requires response within 30 days (with possibility of extension). Specifying the 30-day standard would bring the policy into direct alignment with applicable law.

### No mention of photography consent forms
The [[2.1 Safe Sport, Conduct, and Athlete Protection]] policy (Section E) requires consent for photos/videos of minors. This policy does not reference a corresponding data collection/consent process for these consent forms themselves — i.e., the consent records are personal data that must be managed under this policy, but there's no explicit link.

## Summary

### Purpose
Protect personal information of FENB members, employees, and partners in compliance with applicable privacy legislation. Ensure personal data is collected, stored, used, and shared responsibly for legitimate organizational purposes only. Promotes transparency and accountability; safeguards against unauthorized access or misuse.

### Scope
All personal information handled by FENB: collected through registration, events, memberships, or communications. Applies to Board members, staff, coaches, officials, volunteers, and contractors who access, process, or manage personal data.

### Key Provisions

**Collection and Use**
- Collect personal information only as necessary for administration, membership management, and communications
- Individuals informed of purpose at collection; information not used for secondary purposes without consent or legal requirement

**Storage, Access, and Security**
- Reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure
- Stored securely in password-protected systems or locked files accessible only to authorized personnel
- Electronic communications containing personal data must comply with [[2.1 Safe Sport, Conduct, and Athlete Protection]] and data-protection laws

**Disclosure and Third-Party Access**
- May be disclosed to third parties (insurers, Sport NB, CFF) only for legitimate operational purposes under conditions of confidentiality
- FENB will not sell, trade, or share personal data for marketing or non-sport-related purposes
- Consent obtained whenever possible before third-party release, except as required by law

**Member and Participant Rights**
- Individuals have the right to access their personal information and request correction of inaccuracies
- Requests submitted in writing to the ED; response within a reasonable timeframe

**Retention and Disposal**
- Information retained only as long as necessary for its purpose or to meet legal requirements
- Securely deleted, shredded, or anonymized when no longer required
- Retention practices align with [[2.6 Financial Management]] and audit obligations (7-year minimum for financial records)

**Breach Response and Reporting**
- In the event of a data breach: promptly contain breach, assess impacts, notify affected individuals and authorities as required by law
- ED documents the incident, actions taken, and corrective measures to prevent recurrence
