## Observations

### The Carver financial safety net (EDB-B1) is absent from this policy #gap
The [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]] (EDB-B1) requires that FENB maintain cash reserves of no less than one year's ED salary plus general administrative costs without Board approval. This specific threshold does not appear in the Financial Management Policy. A reader of 2.6 alone would not know about this Board-level constraint. The next policy review should either incorporate the EDB-B1 threshold into 2.6 or explicitly cross-reference the Board Policy Manual.

### Treasurer role has significant independent authority
The Treasurer independently monitors cash flow, reconciles bank statements, and reports irregularities. This role is not otherwise defined in any policy — the scope, selection, and accountability of the Treasurer are implicitly a Board committee matter governed by the Bylaws, but there is no formal Treasurer's mandate in the policy manual.

### "Annual external review or audit as determined by the Board" is discretionary
The policy says external review or audit is determined by the Board — it's not mandatory. For a federally-recognized sport organization receiving provincial funding, the absence of a mandatory audit threshold (e.g., "if revenue exceeds $X") could be a concern, particularly given the growing school program and potential for grant-funded HP programs.

## Summary

### Purpose
Promote sound financial stewardship, accountability, and transparency. Establish controls to ensure funds are managed responsibly, budgets are aligned with organizational goals, and reporting meets applicable standards.

### Scope
All financial activities: budgeting, revenue, expenditures, banking, grants, fundraising, audit processes. Covers Board, ED, staff, and committees involved in financial decision-making.

### Key Provisions

**Financial Planning and Budgeting**
- Annual budget approved by Board before start of fiscal year
- ED and Treasurer prepare draft budget based on program priorities and anticipated revenues
- Reallocation of funds exceeding **10% of an approved line item requires prior Board approval**

**Revenue and Fund Management**
- Accurate records of all revenue sources: membership fees, grants, donations, sponsorships, event income
- All funds deposited into designated organizational bank accounts; recorded per not-for-profit accounting principles
- Fundraising and sponsorship must align with organization's mission and values per [[2.1 Safe Sport, Conduct, and Athlete Protection]]

**Expenditures and Approvals**
- All expenditures must be approved in advance with original receipts/documentation
- **Dual-signature requirement**: ED and one Board member must jointly authorize all payments and cheques
- Cash advances and reimbursements for legitimate FENB business only; reconciled **within 30 days**

**Financial Reporting and Audit**
- Treasurer presents **quarterly financial statements** to Board; annual financial report to membership at AGM
- Accounts subject to **annual external review or audit** as determined by Board
- Audit report and recommendations retained for **minimum 7 years**; used to improve internal controls

**Financial Controls and Accountability**
- Internal controls to prevent fraud, errors, and unauthorized use of funds
- Treasurer monitors cash flow, reconciles bank statements, reports irregularities immediately
- Financial records must be accurate, complete, and accessible to authorized individuals

**Conflict of Interest**
- All Board members and staff involved in financial decisions must comply with [[2.14 Conflict of Interest and Ethics]]
- Individuals must declare real or perceived conflicts prior to financial discussions or votes
