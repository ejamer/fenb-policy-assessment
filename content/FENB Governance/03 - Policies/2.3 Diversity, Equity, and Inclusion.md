## Observations

### The 40% Board gender target faces a difficult pipeline #gap
As of June 2023, FENB's membership is approximately 72% male / 27% female. The DEI policy sets a minimum 40% gender representation target for the Board. This is laudable but the pipeline challenge is real — if the general membership is less than 30% female, drawing Board candidates and leaders from underrepresented genders will require active, specific effort. The [[Strategic Plan 2024-2028]] (Strategy 1) targets +30% gender diversity by 2027-28 as a membership-level goal, which, if achieved, would make the Board target more reachable.

### "Wherever feasible" weakens the Board composition commitment
The 40% Board gender target includes the qualifier "wherever feasible" — which gives the Board a wide exit ramp. For a stronger commitment, future revisions might specify what happens when the target isn't met, or establish a reporting threshold.

### No mention of Indigenous reconciliation
The policy defines equity-seeking groups to include Indigenous Peoples, but unlike the 2SLGBTQ+ and gender dimensions, there are no specific reconciliation-oriented commitments or actions. In a provincial context where Indigenous communities are present, this may be a gap worth addressing.

### Equitable pay mention is noteworthy
The reference to "equitable pay and resource allocation for programs involving men, women, and non-binary athletes" is a notable commitment for a small provincial sport organization. It aligns with Sport Canada's gender equity framework but implies that FENB tracks program investment by participant gender — it's not clear that this tracking mechanism exists.

## Summary

### Purpose
Foster an equitable, diverse, and inclusive environment ensuring equal access to participation and leadership. Removes barriers, prevents discrimination, and promotes representation. Aligns with Sport Canada's national framework for equity, diversity, and inclusion.

### Scope
All FENB members: athletes, coaches, officials, volunteers, staff, Board members. All decisions, programs, activities under FENB jurisdiction, including affiliated clubs.

### Key Definitions
- **Diversity** — wide range of human qualities and attributes (race, ethnicity, gender identity, age, ability, sexual orientation, religion, socioeconomic status)
- **Equity** — fair treatment, access, and opportunity; identifying and eliminating barriers
- **Equity-Seeking Groups** — communities facing collective barriers: Indigenous Peoples, racialized/visible minorities, persons with disabilities, 2SLGBTQ+ individuals
- **Inclusion** — environments where any individual or group feels welcomed, respected, supported, valued to fully participate
- **Gender Equity** — allocating resources, programs, and decision-making fairly across all genders without discrimination

### Key Provisions

**Inclusion and Equity (Principle 1)**
- Welcoming, safe, accessible environment for all backgrounds and identities
- Non-discrimination on any grounds including gender identity, race, or ability
- Equity vs. equality distinction: different programs/resource allocations to meet specific group needs
- Equitable pay and resource allocation for programs involving men, women, and non-binary athletes

**Representation and Leadership (Principle 2)**
- **Gender Balance Goal: minimum 40% representation of each gender on Board of Directors and committees wherever feasible**
- Inclusive recruitment and nomination processes encouraging underrepresented groups
- Mentorship and leadership development for diverse members

**Inclusive Programming and Communications (Principle 3)**
- Programs and competitions accessible to all, including persons with disabilities and marginalized communities
- Event planning accounts for accessibility standards
- Language and imagery in communications will be gender-inclusive and respectful

**Education and Awareness (Principle 4)**
- Ongoing education on gender equity, unconscious bias, and inclusive practices for staff, Board, and volunteers
- Education initiatives align with Safe Sport and HR policies

**Partnerships (Principle 5)**
- Collaborate with CFF, Sport NB, and Sport Canada on national inclusion and gender-equity strategies

**Accountability**
- ED monitors this policy and reports progress to Board annually and to membership at AGM
- Incidents of discrimination/harassment addressed promptly under anti-harassment and Safe Sport policies
