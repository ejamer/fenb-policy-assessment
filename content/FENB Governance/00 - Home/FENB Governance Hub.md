## Key Issues at a Glance

> These are the headline findings from initial analysis. See individual analysis notes for detail.

1. **Stale cross-references** — Policies 2.11 and 2.17 reference "Communications and Social Media Policy (Section 2.1)" which no longer exists as a standalone. Section 2.1 was consolidated into the Safe Sport policy in April 2026; cross-references in other policies were not all updated. See [[Policy Cross-Reference Map]].

2. **Policy numbering gaps** — Sections 2.4, 2.8, 2.9, 2.15, 2.16 are unaccounted for. Section 2.4 is explicitly referenced in the Official Language policy. See [[Policy Inventory and Gap Register]].

3. **Three definitions of the ED role** — The Bylaws define the ED narrowly (records/books/seal). The Carver Board Policy Manual grants broad operational authority. The 12 operational policies assign 15+ specific accountabilities. These are not contradictory but they are unreconciled. See [[ED Authority and Accountability]].

4. **Carver monitoring schedule not connected to operational policies** — The Board Policy Manual (p. 12) specifies a monitoring schedule (semi-annual, quarterly, annual) for ED performance. None of the operational policies reference this schedule. See [[Governance Architecture]].

5. **Strategic Plan targets without policy homes** — The ADC, HP program, coach/referee certification pathways, and armourer program all appear in the Strategic Plan but have no corresponding policy framework. See [[Strategic Plan vs Policy Alignment]].

6. **Safe Sport consolidation artifacts** — Policy 2.1 consolidated at least four prior policies in April 2026 (Safe Sport, Code of Conduct, Anti-Harassment, Communications). The consolidation was not fully propagated across all cross-references in the policy manual. See [[Policy Cross-Reference Map]].
