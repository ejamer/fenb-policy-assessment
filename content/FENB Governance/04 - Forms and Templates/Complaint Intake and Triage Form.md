# Fencing-Escrime NB — Complaint Intake & Triage Form

*Annex to [[2.18 Internal Dispute Resolution]]. This form must be completed by any individual (the "Complainant") wishing to initiate a formal process under FENB policies.*

## Related Policies
- [[2.18 Internal Dispute Resolution]]
- [[2.11 Privacy and Data Protection]]

## Observations on This Form

### Panel composition constraint in practice #gap
The triage guide requires that no panel member be from the same club as either party. With 6 clubs and many individuals serving multiple roles, this constraint may be difficult to satisfy in cases involving members from the largest clubs (DAM: 49 members, RST: 66 members). Pre-identifying a roster of trained, neutral panelists — potentially including individuals from Sport NB or other provinces — would make the 14-day decision timeline feasible.

### No confidentiality acknowledgment section
The form does not include a confidentiality acknowledgment for the Complainant. Adding a brief statement that the complainant agrees to keep the process confidential would align with the [[2.18 Internal Dispute Resolution]] policy's confidentiality principle and set appropriate expectations.

### The form is embedded in the policy — not a standalone document
The Complaint Intake Form is the annex to Policy 2.18, not a separate form document. This means it cannot be updated without formally amending the policy. For a form that should be relatively easy to iterate on (e.g., adding a digital submission option), this coupling is awkward. The form could be referenced by the policy rather than embedded in it.

## Internal Triage Guide

*For FENB Leadership Only — Not for Complainant*

### Step 1 — Jurisdictional Check (The 24-Hour Rule)

Within **24 hours** of receipt, the ED must determine:

| Category Checked | Action Required |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Category A** (or suspected) | **DO NOT INVESTIGATE INTERNALLY.** Forward immediately to NBSSCM (sportnb.com/nb-safe-sport-complaint-mechanism/) or Abuse-Free Sport portal. Inform complainant of referral. |
| **Category B** | File stays with FENB. Proceed to Step 2. |
| **Category C** | Refer to Directoire Technique (DT) or FENB Technical Committee. |

> **Important:** If the category is unclear, treat as Category A and refer externally. It is always safer to refer than to investigate internally something that should go to NBSSCM.

### Step 2 — Conflict of Interest Check

Before proceeding with an internal review, verify that no proposed Review Panel member:
- Is from the same club as the Complainant OR Respondent
- Has a documented personal history with the Complainant or Respondent
- Has any other interest in the outcome

If no panel of 3 can be assembled free of conflicts, consult the Board about bringing in an external panelist (e.g., from Sport NB or another provincial sport organization).

### Step 3 — Notification

- Inform the Respondent of the complaint (redacted for privacy where necessary)
- Give Respondent **7 days** to provide a written response
- Initiate Stage 1 (Informal / Mediation) within **7 days** of the dispute

### Step 4 — Path B Timeline (if Stage 1 fails)

| Stage | Timeline | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Complaint received | Day 0 | Jurisdictional check within 24 hours |
| Respondent notified | Day 1-2 | Written response requested within 7 days |
| Stage 1 Mediation | Within 7 days | Attempt informal resolution |
| If Stage 1 fails: formal complaint | — | Board appoints 3-person Review Panel |
| Stage 2 Hearing | — | Both parties submit evidence; witnesses called |
| Stage 2 Decision | Within **14 days of hearing** | Written decision with reasoning to all parties |

### Step 5 — Recordkeeping

- File all documentation (form, evidence, panel decisions) per [[2.11 Privacy and Data Protection]]
- Brief the Board on the outcome (without personally identifying information, unless required)
- If the matter involves the ED, the President of the Board receives the complaint and manages the process
