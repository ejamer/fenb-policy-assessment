## Priority 2 — Before the April 2028 Policy Review

These are substantive policy gaps that should be addressed in the next review cycle.

### Missing Policies to Draft

- [ ] **Draft Policy 2.4 — Equipment and School Program** — The school program grew from 0 to 68 members in one year (23% of membership). These participants operate under a policy that doesn't exist and have no voting rights. This is the most pressing policy gap. Source: [[Policy Inventory and Gap Register]], [[2.5 Official Language]]
- [ ] **Draft an Athlete Development Policy** — The Athlete Development Representative is a formal Board role (Bylaws §18); the ADC is referenced in the Strategic Plan but has no policy mandate or defined composition. Source: [[Strategic Plan vs Policy Alignment]], [[Bylaws (Nov 2024)]]
- [ ] **Draft a Communications Policy** — Section E of 2.1 provides governance guardrails for communications; a standalone policy would support Strategy 2 (public awareness) and clarify the Webmaster/Communications Officer role. Source: [[Strategic Plan vs Policy Alignment]]
- [ ] **Clarify / resolve sections 2.8 and 2.9** — Two consecutive numbers with no known content. Were these formerly named policies that were merged or removed? Should any content be assigned to them? Source: [[Policy Inventory and Gap Register]]
- [ ] **Clarify / resolve sections 2.15 and 2.16** — Same question as 2.8/2.9. Candidate subjects: selection/eligibility policy, HP program policy. Source: [[Policy Inventory and Gap Register]]

### Policy Strengthening

- [ ] **Add financial safety net threshold to 2.6** — The Board Policy Manual (EDB-B1) requires minimum cash reserves of one year's ED salary + admin costs. This constraint does not appear in the Financial Management Policy (2.6). Either add a cross-reference to EDB-B1 or incorporate the threshold explicitly. Source: [[2.6 Financial Management]], [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]]
- [ ] **Add monitoring schedule cross-reference to 2.6 and 2.2** — The Carver monitoring cadence (quarterly financial, semi-annual member treatment, etc.) is invisible to anyone reading only the operational policies. Source: [[Governance Architecture]], [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]]
- [ ] **Define "nominal value" threshold for gift disclosure in 2.14** — The gift disclosure obligation is triggered by gifts "exceeding a nominal value" but the threshold is undefined. Specify a dollar amount (e.g., $25). Source: [[2.14 Conflict of Interest and Ethics]]
- [ ] **Specify 30-day access request timeline in 2.11** — The Privacy policy says requests will be responded to "within a reasonable timeframe." PIPEDA requires 30 days. Aligning the policy language to the legal standard would remove ambiguity. Source: [[2.11 Privacy and Data Protection]]
- [ ] **Reconcile 2.7 HR grievance process with 2.18 IDR** — Both policies describe internal grievance/conflict resolution. Confirm they are the same process or explain when each applies. Source: [[Policy Cross-Reference Map]]
- [ ] **Fix "Board Governance and Accountability Guidelines (if applicable)" reference in 2.14** — Either locate and link this document or remove the reference. The "(if applicable)" qualifier suggests it may not exist. Source: [[2.14 Conflict of Interest and Ethics]]

### Missing Operational Documents

- [ ] **Create a standard Conflict of Interest Declaration Form** — Referenced in both the Board Policy Manual (GP-D1) and Policy 2.14. Required annually of all Board members and senior staff. Should be versioned. Source: [[Referenced but Absent Documents]]
- [ ] **Create a Conflict of Interest Register and Gift Log template** — Required by Policy 2.14 but no format exists. Source: [[Referenced but Absent Documents]]
- [ ] **Version-control the FENB Pay Scale** — Currently held in an unversioned Google Doc (linked in 2.7 HR). Changes can be made without formal governance approval. Import a versioned copy into this vault or establish an approval process for changes. Source: [[2.7 Human Resources]], [[Referenced but Absent Documents]]
- [ ] **Create a Risk Register template** — Policy 2.2 requires the ED to maintain an annual risk register. No format is specified. Source: [[2.2 Risk Management]]
- [ ] **Add a confidentiality acknowledgment to the Complaint Intake Form** — The form (annex to 2.18) does not ask the complainant to acknowledge the confidentiality obligations of the process. Source: [[Complaint Intake and Triage Form]]

## Priority 3 — At Next Bylaws Amendment Cycle

These require member-level action (2/3 vote) and cannot be addressed by Board resolution alone.

- [ ] **Amend Bylaws §19 to reflect actual ED authority** — The Bylaws describe the ED as a record-keeper. The Carver model grants full operational authority. Adding a general clause ("the ED serves as the organization's chief operating officer with authority delegated by the Board per the Board Policy Manual") would align the legal document with actual governance practice. Source: [[Bylaws (Nov 2024)]], [[ED Authority and Accountability]]
- [ ] **Consider voting rights for Special Recreational / School members** — School members now represent 23% of participants and have no voting rights. As this segment grows, the democratic legitimacy concern grows with it. The Bylaws could be amended to create a path to voting rights after a defined period of participation. Source: [[Bylaws (Nov 2024)]], [[Strategic Plan 2024-2028]]
- [ ] **Formalize the Athlete Development Committee in the Bylaws** — Currently optional ("if one exists" per §18). Given its strategic importance, making the ADC a standing committee with defined composition and mandate would strengthen its governance foundation. Source: [[Bylaws (Nov 2024)]]

## Priority 4 — Board Policy Manual Revision

These require a Board resolution to amend. Recommended for the next Board Policy Manual revision cycle.

- [ ] **Align GP-A4 (Board Inclusivity) with the 40% gender target in Policy 2.3** — GP-A4 says "reasonable efforts"; Policy 2.3 specifies a minimum 40% target. The operational policy is more specific and should be the operative standard; the Board Policy Manual should reflect it. Source: [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]], [[2.3 Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion]]
- [ ] **Update EDB-A7 (Freedom from Abuse) to cross-reference Policy 2.1** — EDB-A7 is one sentence. The Safe Sport framework is now a comprehensive policy. The Board Policy Manual should at minimum reference 2.1 rather than restating a narrow version. Source: [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]]
- [ ] **Conduct a full alignment review between Board Policy Manual and April 2026 operational policies** — The Board Policy Manual was last substantively revised in 2017. The 2026 policies are more developed; the two layers need a reconciliation pass. Source: [[Governance Architecture]]
