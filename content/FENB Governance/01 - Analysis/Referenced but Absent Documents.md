# Referenced but Absent Documents

This note catalogues every document that is referenced in the FENB governance framework but is not present in this vault. These represent operational gaps that should be addressed.

---

## Category 1 — Critical Operational Documents (High Priority)

### FENB Crisis Management Protocol
- **Referenced in:** [[2.17 Crisis and Emergency Management]] — referenced 4 times as the operational companion to the policy
- **What it should contain:** Emergency response procedures; communication chain; roles and responsibilities during a crisis; post-crisis review process
- **Why it matters:** Policy 2.17 is almost entirely a wrapper pointing to this Protocol. Without it, the "crisis management" framework has no operational content. The policy says the ED "will maintain" the Protocol — present tense of a future obligation, suggesting it may not have existed at April 2026 approval.
- **Status:** Unknown — may be in development; may have existed as part of the old Operations Manual (archived in the source repo)
- **Note:** The archived Operations Manual (`operations-manual-en.pdf` and `operations-manual-fr.pdf`) in the source repo may contain the predecessor to this Protocol. These were not read as part of this vault's initial population.

### Conflict of Interest Declaration Form
- **Referenced in:** [[2.14 Conflict of Interest and Ethics]] ("Annual conflict-of-interest declarations must be completed by all Board members and senior staff"); [[Board Policy Manual (Feb 2024)]] GP-D1 ("completing a Conflict of Interest Declaration")
- **What it should contain:** A structured annual declaration form capturing potential conflicts; signature and date; ED/Board retention
- **Why it matters:** This form is required annually of all Board members and senior staff. Its absence from the vault means there is no standard template — declarations may be inconsistent in scope and completeness.
- **Status:** Likely exists informally; should be formalized and versioned.

### Conflict of Interest Register / Gift Log
- **Referenced in:** [[2.14 Conflict of Interest and Ethics]] ("FENB will maintain a register of disclosed conflicts and gifts, accessible to the Board")
- **Why it matters:** The register is described as a Board oversight tool. Without a defined format, it may exist only in the ED's files or may not exist at all.
- **Status:** Unknown.

### Policy 2.4 — Equipment and School Program Policy

### Board Governance and Accountability Guidelines
- **Referenced in:** [[2.14 Conflict of Interest and Ethics]] Related Policies section as "(if applicable)"
- **Why it matters:** The "(if applicable)" qualifier is unusual — it may mean this document was planned but not created, or it may exist but FENB is unsure whether it applies. This should be clarified.
